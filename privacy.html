Privacy policy

Draft for owner review — not in effect. The application is under development. Hosting, retention and deletion procedures must be confirmed before live use. This draft does not authorize collection.

Shivers Racing Labs (SRL) is the independent developer of SRL Hidden Tech, a Discord application designed to deliver curated motorsport engineering articles. This policy describes the proposed initial service and its planned data handling.

1. Information the application is designed to process

Discord provides server and channel identifiers, interaction details (including the invoking user identifier and applicable permissions), and limited message metadata needed for commands and delivery. The initial test bot handles a test-server command without a persistent user database. Planned publishing features would store server and selected channel IDs, schedule/time zone, enabled status, article IDs, delivery timestamps and bot-generated message IDs. Operational logs may include timestamps, error details and service identifiers. A future delivery recovery mechanism may read recent messages in the selected channel solely to identify its own posts.

SRL does not intend to collect member lists, monitor general conversations, profile members, use advertising trackers, or request privileged gateway intents. Discord may still supply information in command interactions or API responses; the application should discard unnecessary data and not intentionally store unrelated messages.

2. Purpose and sharing

Data is intended to support command responses, permission checks, article delivery, duplicate prevention, service security and troubleshooting. Discord and a selected cloud hosting provider may process information as needed for operation. SRL does not intend to sell API data or use it for advertising or AI training. Disclosure may occur where required by law or necessary to address security incidents. The hosting provider and relevant processing details must be confirmed before launch.

3. Retention, removal and user rights

Proposed retention targets: server settings and delivery history while installed; removal of associated active records within 30 days of a verified removal or deletion request, operational logs for up to 30 days and rotating backups for up to 30 days thereafter, subject to applicable legal or security obligations. These are design targets, not yet implemented or verified. Users should be able to request access, correction or deletion of their data, and exercise applicable privacy rights through a private support route. Removing the bot does not automatically remove posts already delivered to Discord; server administrators can manage those messages.

4. Security and incidents

SRL plans to use restricted service permissions, protected credentials, limited access to storage and secure connections. No system can guarantee absolute security. SRL will follow applicable incident notification requirements, including Discord's developer requirements.

5. Website and third parties

This static documentation site contains no SRL analytics scripts, advertising scripts, forms or intentional browser storage. GitHub hosts the site and its avatar image, and may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. Discord handles use of its platform under its privacy policy. External technical sources have their own policies.

6. Contact and updates

For support, privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact SRL privately at shiversracinglabs@gmail.com. A final effective date must be added before this policy becomes active. Do not submit private data or credentials through public GitHub issues. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy. The application will link to its final policy before live use.
